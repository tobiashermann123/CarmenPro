## Fundamental Overview

XRP's "fundamentals" are a hybrid between crypto-native token economics and enterprise software adoption metrics for RippleNet.

**Regulatory Clarity (Key Differentiator):** The July 2024 Ripple vs SEC ruling established that programmatic sales of XRP on exchanges do not constitute securities offerings. This removed the primary regulatory overhang that had suppressed XRP since December 2020. Ripple paid a $125M civil penalty (vs SEC's demand for $2B+) — a resolution widely seen as a major win. The SEC dropped its appeal in 2025.

**RippleNet Adoption:** Ripple claims 300+ financial institution partnerships across 55+ countries using RippleNet for cross-border payments. However, actual on-ledger XRP usage from these partnerships has been limited — many use RippleNet messaging without settling in XRP. The ODL (On-Demand Liquidity) product that actually uses XRP as a bridge currency has seen growing volume, particularly in USD/MXN, USD/PHP corridors.

**Token Economics:** Total XRP supply is 100 billion (pre-mined). Ripple holds ~45 billion in escrow, releasing up to 1 billion per month. This controlled release creates predictable supply inflation of ~0.3% monthly — a headwind vs Bitcoin's diminishing supply. However, Ripple has been burning/retiring tokens selectively.

**XRPL NFT and DeFi ecosystem:** The XRPL has expanded into NFTs and DeFi (XRPL AMM launched 2024), adding utility beyond payments. Total Value Locked on XRPL DEX has grown to $350M+.

**Comparison to SWIFT:** SWIFT processes ~$5T/day in cross-border transactions. XRP Ledger processes ~50,000 TPS, settles in 3–5 seconds, with near-zero fees. The technological case is clear; the adoption case is slower than bulls projected. SWIFT's Connector service is directly competing, and Ripple must compete against an entrenched 40-year-old network.
